## 🇨🇳 中文

电商活动策划专家，根据你的指令或素材产出包含正文、图片与表格的完整活动策划方案。默认交付一份已经本地落地、可用浏览器打开验证的 HTML 网页版方案，内部能研讨、对上能汇报、跨部门能拆解、活动后能复盘。覆盖淘宝/天猫、抖音电商、京东、得物、拼多多、小红书、Amazon、TikTok Shop、Shopee、Lazada、eBay、Shopify/独立站等平台的节点大促、店铺活动、平台活动、直播间活动、品类活动、品牌日、上新、清仓、会员复购、内容种草到成交、站内外联动与跨境电商活动。方案会串起背景与目标、人群、货品、促销机制、流量、店铺运营、预算、履约保障到复盘闭环的连续策略主线。价格、折扣、库存、销量、预算、日期等均要求有可靠依据，不编造数字、链接或功效；若你只要商品标题详情页文案或单张商业图片，它会引导你改用对应技能。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入平台、品类、节点、预算与历史数据 → 输出含背景目标、人群、货品、促销机制、流量、预算、履约与复盘闭环的完整方案
- 默认交付一份本地落地、可浏览器打开验证的 HTML 网页版方案，正文、图片、表格齐全并按实际数据配置必要图表
- 覆盖 13 类平台场景：节点大促、店铺活动、平台活动、直播间活动、品类活动、品牌日、上新、清仓、会员复购、内容种草到成交、站内外联动与跨境电商
- 价格、折扣、库存、销量、预算、日期、平台资源位与专业结论均需可靠依据，不编造数字、链接或功效
- 定量目标须完成品牌历史、行业/品类与可比案例对标，并标注来源、时间、口径、推导方式与置信度
- 完整方案至少落位 2 个活动视觉（重视觉方案优先 4 或 6 个），图片优先使用用户提供、官方或授权素材

## 🇬🇧 English

An e-commerce campaign planning expert that turns your brief or materials into a complete campaign plan with body copy, images and tables. By default it delivers a locally written HTML page you can open and verify in a browser, ready for internal review, reporting to leadership, cross-team breakdown and post-campaign review. It covers node mega-sales, store, platform, livestream, category, brand-day, launch, clearance, member-repurchase, content-to-conversion, on/off-site联动 and cross-border campaigns across Taobao/Tmall, Douyin e-commerce, JD, Dewu, Pinduoduo, Xiaohongshu, Amazon, TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada, eBay and Shopify/independent sites. Each plan carries one continuous strategy line from background and goals through audience, assortment, promotion mechanics, traffic, store operations and budget to fulfillment guarantees and a review loop. Prices, discounts, inventory, sales, budgets and dates must all have reliable sourcing — no invented numbers, links or efficacy claims.

### ✨ What it does

- Feed in platform, category, campaign node, budget and historical data → get one plan covering goals, audience, assortment, promo mechanics, traffic, budget, fulfillment and a review loop
- Ships by default as a locally written HTML page you can open in a browser, with body copy, images and tables plus charts configured from the actual data
- Covers 13 platform scenarios: node mega-sales, store, platform, livestream, category, brand-day, launch, clearance, member repurchase, content-to-conversion, on/off-site linkage and cross-border campaigns
- Prices, discounts, inventory, sales, budgets, dates, platform placements and conclusions all require reliable sourcing — no invented numbers, links or efficacy claims
- Quantitative targets must be benchmarked against brand history, industry/category data and comparable cases, with source, date, scope, derivation and confidence noted
- Every complete plan carries at least 2 campaign visuals (4 or 6 for visual-heavy plans), preferring user-provided, official or licensed assets
